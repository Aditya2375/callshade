01 / THE READING DESK

One sentence at a time.

This is a small, synthetic teaching model, not production financial NLP. Pasted text stays in this page. It can be wrong, especially on negation and unfamiliar language.

Up to 16,000 Unicode characters, 160 sentences, 600 characters and 128 ASCII word tokens per sentence. Headers must stand alone: [Prepared], [Q&A], CEO:, CFO:, Analyst:, Operator:.

No file upload, feed, storage, cookies or external model. Reset or close this tab to clear text. Static hosting may log asset requests.

02 / A SMALL MODEL, OPEN BOOKS

Learn the counts.
Show the limits.

No opaque service and no licensed lexicon hidden underneath. Original Python and JavaScript implementations fit the same multinomial naive-Bayes model.

01 / TOKENIZE

Words, then pairs.

Lowercase ASCII word tokens and adjacent bigrams. Numbers and punctuation are not features. No stemming, semantic parsing or explicit negation logic.

02 / FIT

Train on invented calls.

54 author-labeled sentences from calls 01 to 09 set the vocabulary, class priors and smoothed feature counts. Nothing from the excerpt refits the model.

03 / ASSIGN

Add the log evidence.

Sum class log prior and recognized feature counts times learned log weights. Largest score wins; class-order ties prefer negative, then neutral, then positive.

04 / QUESTION

Inspect, do not trust.

Zero known features means unscored, not neutral. A tiny familiar fragment can still force a label. Correlated word pairs can make evidence look stronger than it is.

Open the synthetic holdout audit

Calls 10 to 12 are held out in full: 18 invented sentences, 6 per class. Labels describe the author's intended tone, not price outcomes. Calls share vocabulary and writing patterns with training. Even a perfect score here does not establish real-world accuracy. This is a fixed group split, not time validation or cross-validation. Changing smoothing after reading these results is exploration, not untouched testing.

Majority-class baseline is 6/18 = 33.3%. Tied training class counts choose negative. Macro-F1 averages the three class F1 values; unscored predictions count as misses.

03 / WHAT A LABEL CANNOT SAY

Language is context.
This is a baseline.

It does not understand the call.

Questions, sarcasm, scope of negation, comparisons, numbers, forward-looking language and speaker intent are not understood. Sentence splitting is simple, not an earnings-transcript parser. Non-ASCII language is not supported; accented words can fragment into misleading ASCII pieces.

It has not been validated on real companies.

72 short invented sentences cannot establish performance on real financial disclosures. No FinBERT, Loughran-McDonald dictionary, copied transcript, outside model weights or benchmark results are included or claimed.

It is not a market signal.

No stock data, returns, trading rule, broker integration, PnL backtest, investment advice or forecast. Model labels are fallible descriptions of text, not evidence about a business or its price.

It stays here.

Text runs locally in this page. No telemetry, uploads, storage, cookies or runtime network. The hosting service may log ordinary asset requests. Source links leave the page.
